🎓 1. Course Categories (LearnPress LMS) Because Seat of Wisdom is integrated natively with the LearnPress LMS plugin, its core content revolves around academic courses. You can group courses by: Academic Departments: e.g., Science, Humanities, Mathematics, Technology. Education Levels: e.g., Preschool, Primary School, High School, Undergraduate. Skill / Topic Sectors: e.g., Language Learning, Digital Literacy, Arts & Crafts.

📅 2. Event Categories Schools use this custom post type to keep parents, students, and staff updated. Events are filtered by: Academic Calendar: e.g., Term Exams, Holidays, Parent-Teacher Meetings. Co-Curricular / Social: e.g., Sports Day, Science Fairs, Music Festivals. Admissions: e.g., Open House, Orientation Programs.

🏢 3. Research & Department CategoriesFor larger schools or higher education institutes, Eikra features layout templates specific to academic study: Research Fields: e.g., Biotechnology, Engineering, Social Sciences. Publications: e.g., Journals, Institutional Papers, Student Magazines.

📝 4. Blog & News CategoriesThis is standard WordPress categorization used to run the school's communication portal: Campus News: Daily updates, principal statements, and announcements. Student Achievements: Spotlighting competition winners and academic honors. Educational Resources: Articles, study tips, and resources written by teachers.

🛍️ 5. Shop Categories (WooCommerce)Seat of Wisdom includes full WooCommerce integration, allowing school websites to sort purchasable items into distinct product categories: School Supplies: Textbooks, backpacks, and stationary. Merchandise: School uniforms, sports jerseys, and branded gear. Digital Resources: E-books, lesson plan templates, and tuition fee packages.
